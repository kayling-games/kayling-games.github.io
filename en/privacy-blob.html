Privacy policy for the app “Blob.io”

2. Summary

  • You don't need an account to play. We don't ask for your name, email address or any other details.
  • Your progress is stored only locally on your device and is not sent to us.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner in the menu and pause screen, occasionally a full-screen ad between two worlds, and an optional video for the “second chance”. For this, Google processes data from your device as described in section 4.
  • Personalised ads are only shown with your consent. You can change your choice in the game at any time.

3. Progress on your device

The app saves your progress, points, best score, unlocked hats and settings (for example sound, vibration, joystick) in your device's local storage. This data does not leave your device. The names in the leaderboard are made up and are also generated on the device only. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Storing it is technically necessary for the game (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

4. Advertising with Google AdMob

The app uses the ad service Google AdMob provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). Ads appear in three places: as a banner at the bottom of the main menu and the pause screen (never while you play), at most every three minutes as a full-screen ad when moving to the next world or starting a new run, and as a video when you tap “Second chance (video)” yourself after being eaten.

Data processed

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID, or on iPhone the IDFA, the latter only with your permission, see section 5)
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, for example device model, operating system, language, app version
  • Information about your interaction with ads, for example whether an ad was shown, watched to the end or tapped
  • Crash and diagnostic data of the ads SDK

Purposes

Serving and measuring ads, billing advertisers, detecting and preventing fraud and abuse and, if you consent, personalised ads based on your interests.

Legal bases

For personalised ads and the related access to information on your device we ask for your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Without consent, Google only shows non-personalised or limited ads. Non-personalised ads also use information on your device (for example to count how often an ad has been shown); we ask for your consent for this in the same dialog (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). If you decline everything, Google only shows limited ads that work without the advertising ID and without access to your device's storage. The processing needed for this (serving the ad, billing, detecting fraud and invalid clicks) is based on our legitimate interest in financing the free app through advertising and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Showing the consent dialog and storing your choice on the device are technically necessary so that we can respect it (Art. 6(1)(c) in conjunction with Art. 7 GDPR, Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Recipients and transfers to third countries

The recipient of the data is Google. Google partly processes the data as an independent controller. Data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which an adequacy decision of the European Commission exists (Art. 45 GDPR). Depending on the AdMob settings, Google's ad partners may also deliver ads.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Consent, Apple tracking prompt and withdrawal

On first launch, the app asks via Google's consent solution (User Messaging Platform) whether you agree to personalised ads. On iPhone, Apple also asks whether the app may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline there, the app gets no access to the IDFA. The app only asks if you have not already turned off “Allow Apps to Request to Track” on your iPhone.

The game works fully either way, even if you decline. You will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to you.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with effect for the future:

  • in the game under Settings → Werbe-Einwilligung (ad consent; the button appears where consent is required for your location, for example in the EU),
  • on iPhone also under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking,
  • on Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads (reset or delete the advertising ID; the exact path depends on the device).

7. Retention

We do not store any personal data from the app ourselves. Your progress and your consent choice stay on your device until you change them or delete the app. How long Google stores data is described in Google's privacy policy. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as we need it to deal with your request and then delete it, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Because we do not receive any data from the app ourselves, we usually cannot identify you. For data held by Google, it is best to contact Google directly.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example in your country of residence, or the data protection authority of the German federal state in which the GbR has its registered office.

Contact

For any questions about data protection and to exercise your rights, contact us at the email address or postal address given in section 1.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version on this page applies.
